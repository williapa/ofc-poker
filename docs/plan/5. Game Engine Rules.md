# Prompt 5 - Add legality, royalties, pairwise scoring, and Fantasyland

### Goal

Complete the rules engine according to the rules reference. Evaluate board legality, calculate all front/middle/back royalties, score every unordered player pair using standard 1–6 scoring, preserve zero-sum totals, and determine Fantasyland entry/re-entry and reveal state.

### Acceptance criteria

- Board legality is based on `back >= middle >= front`, with adjacent equality allowed.
- Fouled-versus-legal and fouled-versus-fouled scoring exactly matches the reference.
- Legal pair scoring includes row results, scoop bonus, and net royalties; tied rows score zero.
- All royalty tables and royal-flush distinctions match the reference.
- Two-, three-, and four-player totals are calculated pairwise and always sum to zero.
- Fantasyland qualification, simultaneous Fantasyland players, requalification, face-down state, and showdown reveal follow the documented policies.
- Round output includes per-row evaluations, foul status, royalties, pairwise breakdowns, total deltas, and next-hand Fantasyland status.
- Exhaustive table-driven examples cover royalty boundaries, fouls, ties, scoops, multi-player scoring, and Fantasyland conditions.
