# Prompt 14 - Deliver the complete offline AI-lobby mode

## Goal

Integrate local provider, runner, and AI strategies into a production-quality local-play mode. A human should be able to start immediately with one to three AI opponents, complete multiple hands, and tune internal bot profiles through a centralized configuration without contacting Playroom.

## Acceptance criteria

- AI mode supports a total of two, three, or four seats with exactly one local human and the remaining seats filled by AI.
- Creating and playing an AI lobby never initializes Playroom, opens a WebSocket, or sends a multiplayer-service request; an automated test asserts this boundary.
- AI turns progress without manual intervention, cannot act twice, and pause/stop cleanly when the runner is disposed.
- The UI communicates AI thinking without making automated tests depend on real time.
- Multiple hands preserve scores, dealer rotation, and Fantasyland.
- Strategy/difficulty is configured in one internal registry and can be changed without engine or view edits.
- An integration test plays a full human-plus-AI hand using deterministic cards and validates the resulting score screen.

## Usage

12 percent
